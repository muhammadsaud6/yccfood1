Welcome To Our Table

gabrielhmcknight · Feb 24, 2023 · 2 min read

CEO and head-Chef Gabriel invites you to take a seat at the Your Cuisine Chef table.

Since 2014, I have been servicing schools, businesses and cafes with nutritionists and seasonal dishes. Your Cuisine Chef (YCC) aims to amplify and expand upon our mission to serve excellence through sourcing, service and sustainability.

YCC provides full professional service and on-site preparation of nutritional fare to schools, colleges and special events across the United States. We prepare all food freshly and daily from the best hand-selected ingredients according to their seasonal availability. We hold our standards to the highest level of service, going above and beyond to adhere to each guest's specific needs.

Your school, institution or business is unique and deserves to be managed by a company that treats you as if you were our only client. The YCC team is here to complement your community. Our children only deserve the very best, and it is up to us to deliver that! Our company's primary goal is to exceed the expectations of our independent schools, charter schools, and business dining partners and clients. We serve all grade levels as well as teachers and administrators. Each school is managed by a Chef Manager and supported by a clerical assistant, a manager, and area administrator.

We also serve special event catering for these institutions (graduations, pre-game meals, board meetings, senior birthday celebrations, concessions, holidays, weekend events, etc.) and provide full-service event catering for corporations and other organizations. Our friendly and professional staff is experienced in all facets of foodservice and receives ongoing quarterly training in customer service, culinary training, and food safety. All managers, chefs, clerical assistants, area administrators, and kitchen supervisors have food service manager certifications and are ready to serve you.

Ready to dine with us? Fill out this form so we can work together. Thank you for joining us!
